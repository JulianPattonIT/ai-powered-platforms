## 📌 Overview

Baltimore Gold Fronts is a luxury custom grillz jewelry brand based in Baltimore, MD. This case study documents the end-to-end delivery of a production-grade e-commerce platform — built across 3 milestones, generating **$1,400 in sales within the first month of launch.**

The project was scoped, built, audited, and optimized entirely through **AI-powered development and prompt engineering** — delivering a feature set comparable to agencies charging 3-5x the price.

## ✅ Full Delivery Criteria — 15 of 15

**Booking & Payments**
- 📅 Online booking with deposits via Sesami — automated email reminders included
- 💳 Secure payments — Stripe, PayPal, credit cards, Shop Pay
- 💸 Shop Pay Installments — Buy Now Pay Later financing to drive higher order values
- 🧾 Automated receipts and full payment history in Shopify admin

**Customer Journey**
- 📋 Custom quote & inquiry system — step-by-step flow capturing gold karat, teeth count, style, and extras
- 🛒 Abandoned cart recovery — 3-email Klaviyo sequence for incomplete checkouts
- 📬 Abandoned quote follow-ups — automated recovery for incomplete inquiry forms
- 📦 Order tracking with automated shipping notifications
- ✅ Legal acknowledgment checkbox at checkout

**Storefront & Conversion**
- 🏠 Sales-focused homepage optimized for conversion
- 🔼 Product recommendations and upsells
- 📱 Mobile-optimized checkout and storefront
- 📄 All policy pages — privacy, terms, refund, FAQ

**Marketing & Operations**
- 📲 Instagram Shop + TikTok Shop fully integrated
- 📊 Analytics setup, client training, and full documentation delivered

---

## 🏗️ Key Build Highlights

**Custom Molding Appointment Page**
Built a dedicated luxury-toned appointment page for custom grill molding sessions — capturing deposits, appointment details, and client preferences in a branded experience consistent with the premium positioning of the brand.

**Mobile Page Speed Optimization**
Conducted a full HAR file analysis and Shopify theme audit to resolve mobile performance issues ahead of paid ad campaign launch — ensuring fast load times for traffic driven by Google, Facebook, and Instagram ads.

**Klaviyo Email Automation Architecture**
Designed and implemented a full email automation system covering transactional messages, abandoned cart sequences, abandoned quote recovery, and browse abandonment — creating multiple recovery touchpoints across the customer journey.

**Social Commerce Integration**
Connected the Shopify catalog to both Instagram Shop and TikTok Shop, enabling direct product purchases from social media without leaving the platform.
